Government positions & proposals

National positions

Sovereignty and self-government

The government supports each micronation’s ability to establish its own policies on the use of AI in administration through its own legislative processes. A Greenwick opposition charter has been proposed in response to external attempts to set universal eligibility standards for micronations.

Government updates →

Constitutional discussions

House of Citizens proposal

A proposal to add a middle legislative chamber was put to a referendum campaign in October 2026. No confirmed result or enactment is recorded on this page.
