Announcement

Greenwick proposes charter on micronational sovereignty and AI governance

The Government of Greenwick has announced that it intends to put forward an opposition charter supporting each micronation's authority to determine its own approach to artificial intelligence in governance through its legislature. It opposes rules that permit another nation, group or coalition to determine whether a polity qualifies as a micronation solely on the basis of AI use.
