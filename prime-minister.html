Office of the Prime Minister

Head of Government

The Hon Sean Mannix MP

Prime Minister of the Commonwealth of Greenwick

Sean Mannix is identified in the Commonwealth's 2026 parliamentary records as the sixth Prime Minister of Greenwick. He leads the Teal Conservative Party and assumed the premiership following the resignation of Thomas Delvecchio during a period of political transition.

Responsibilities

The Prime Minister heads the national executive, directs government policy, coordinates ministerial administration and represents the Commonwealth in governmental and diplomatic matters.

Government leadership

The executive also includes Deputy Prime Minister Olivia Hartwell MP and Treasurer William Carrington MP. Ministerial portfolios and government programme information can be found in the national government directory.
