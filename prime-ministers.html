Prime Ministers of Greenwick

The original Prime Ministers page contained no substantive body text in the supplied capture. The historical register below is reconstructed from the Royal Archives and current Prime Minister pages contained in the same archive.

2023–2025

Jakob Furgason

First elected Prime Minister of Veridonia and later Greenwick; led the nation through its foundational years before resigning in 2025.

2025

Rachel Sinclair

Second Prime Minister, elected democratically. The Royal Archives records that her term ended after her assassination.

2025

James Hendricksson

Acting Prime Minister following Rachel Sinclair’s death; remembered in the archive for continuity and constitutional stability.

2026

Thomas Delvecchio

Former Prime Minister referenced across several archived government pages.

2026–present in this archive

Sean Mannix MP

Appointed as the sixth Prime Minister following Thomas Delvecchio’s resignation. Leader of the Teal Conservative Party.
