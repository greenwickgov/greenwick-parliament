Royal Family Members

The Royal Family of the Commonwealth of Greenwick plays a central ceremonial and cultural role in the life of the nation. With a deep commitment to service and tradition, each family member contributes to the unity and strength of the Commonwealth. His Majesty King Ashton I Monarch of Greenwick Reigns as the Head of State Presides over national ceremonies and holidays Issues royal messages and addresses Acts as a unifying figure for all citizens Princess Abby of Elderview Sister of the King Promotes education, youth leadership, and the arts Leads the Royal Initiative for Cultural Engagement (RICE) Supports youth organizations and local schools Prince Zeke of Northbridge Brother of the King Leads environmental and sustainability campaigns Oversees the Greenwick Clean Future Program Represents the Commonwealth in scientific and tech exchanges Duke Toby of Northwell Brother of the King Ambassador for Northern Development Projects Works closely with local councils and youth centers Supports infrastructure and community outreach programs Wayne, Prince of Westmarch Father of the King Serves as ceremonial advisor to the King Maintains historical records and royal protocol Represents the Royal House at official legacy events Kathy, Duchess of Westmarch Mother of the King Head of the Royal Family’s Charitable Trust Supports health, welfare, and women’s initiatives Hosts national and community receptions at the royal estate
