The Senate

Legislative chamber

The upper house of Parliament

The Senate is a chamber of Greenwick's parliamentary system. It provides an additional legislative forum for examining proposed laws, debating national issues and scrutinising government decisions.

Role and responsibilities

Senators take part in legislative debate and parliamentary oversight. Proposed laws proceed according to the Commonwealth's established legislative process.

Parliamentary parties

Greenwick's political history features Labor, Liberal, the Teal Conservative Party and several minor parties and independents. The composition of particular houses may change over election cycles and coalition negotiations. This page does not treat historical election forecasts as the present Senate results.
